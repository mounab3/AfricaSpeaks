## ✨ About the Project

**AfricaSpeaks** is a language-learning platform prototype inspired by the incredible linguistic and cultural diversity of Africa.

Its mission is to help people discover African languages, connect with passionate tutors, and explore the cultures and communities behind every language.

More than a language-learning website, AfricaSpeaks is a growing vision for cultural exchange and connection.

## 🗺️ Explore Africa

Discover the continent through an interactive map and explore the languages associated with different countries.

### What you can discover

- 🗺️ An interactive map of African countries.
- 🔎 Country search and language discovery.
- 🗣️ A growing directory of African languages.
- 📚 Available Arabic and Tunisian Arabic lessons.
- 👩‍🏫 Tutor profiles and learning information.

## 🌱 Our Language Community

Africa is home to extraordinary linguistic diversity. AfricaSpeaks aims to celebrate that diversity and make it more accessible to learners around the world.

Some of the languages we hope to feature include:

| Region | Languages |
|:---|:---|
| North Africa | Arabic, Amazigh |
| West Africa | Wolof, Yoruba, Hausa |
| East Africa | Swahili, Amharic, Oromo |
| Central Africa | Lingala, Kikongo, Fulfulde |
| Southern Africa | Zulu, Xhosa, Shona |

*This is an illustrative selection, not an exhaustive list of African languages.*

## 👩‍🏫 Start Learning

Our first featured learning opportunities are:

- **Arabic** — Explore the language through communication, vocabulary, reading, and writing.
- **Tunisian Arabic** — Discover the Tunisian dialect, everyday expressions, and local communication.

More languages and tutors are part of our future vision.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

AfricaSpeaks combines a warm, welcoming visual identity with a simple and accessible user experience.

- 🤎 Warm, earthy colors inspired by African landscapes.
- ✨ Elegant typography and thoughtful layouts.
- 📱 Responsive design for different screen sizes.
- 🌍 Interactive experiences that encourage exploration.
